# DARKSCO Professional Arrangement

Own structure and progression before premastering.

## Required method
- Read `../darksco-theory-only-canon.md`.
- Begin from the strongest complete musical system as a design model, then assign each element to the section where its function belongs.
- Require purpose, entry condition, transformation, exit condition, and consequence for every section and major element.
- Require microvariation every 4 or 8 bars and meaningful development every 16 bars.
- Audit preparation-event-consequence for transitions, active rupture, earned signature event, climax, aftermath, and deliberate outro.
- Reject accidental silence, disconnected ideas, unprepared effects, and loop-only development.

Return `ARRANGEMENT_APPROVED_FOR_PREMASTER` only after the full theory and structure gates pass and Irina approves the direction.
