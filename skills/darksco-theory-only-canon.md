## Authority
- Irina is the sole final decision authority.

- Current Irina feedback overrides prior production assumptions.

## Instruction reset

- Do not reuse rejected sound palettes, melodies, arrangements, renders, or aesthetic descriptions as creative direction.

## Composition theory
1. Establish a tonal center, modal/scale vocabulary, harmonic tension map, and legal non-chord-tone behavior before detailed rendering.
2. Compose bass and rhythmic identity first when they are the structural anchor. Validate every bass note against harmonic function and voice-leading.
3. Derive principal melodic material from the same interval family, rhythmic cell, or harmonic identity.
4. Limit foreground hierarchy to one principal idea at a time. Counterparts must answer, support, transform, or create intentional tension.
5. Use repetition with development: change articulation, rhythm, register, voicing, orchestration, spatial treatment, omission, or phrase ending.
6. Require bounded microvariation every 4 or 8 bars and a meaningful structural or harmonic change at least every 16 bars.
7. Treat dissonance as prepared, functional, and resolved or deliberately sustained. Never allow accidental out-of-key collisions.

## Arrangement theory
- Build the strongest complete musical system as a design reference, then distribute its components across the timeline according to section function.
- The opening must establish causality and momentum without presenting every completed layer.
- Each section requires purpose, dominant information, energy/density range, entry condition, exit condition, and relationship to earlier material.
- Major transitions require preparation, event, and consequence.
- Ruptures may reduce density but must not create accidental dead air.
- Signature sections and climaxes must be earned by prior development.
- The outro must resolve, suspend, or transfer the central tension deliberately and preserve useful tails when required.

## Sound-design and mix mechanics
- Design dry sources before corrective processing.
- Keep kick, bass, hats, percussion, music, atmosphere, structural FX, impacts/transitions, and material returns independently controllable when present.
- Low-frequency ownership is project-specific and must be documented. Kick and bass overlap only with deliberate timing, filtering, phase control, and sidechain or dynamic separation.
- Filter boundaries are starting hypotheses, not universal constants; validate them by spectrum, phase, transient behavior, and listening.
- Hats require independent transient and high-frequency control.
- Structural FX must manipulate or respond to the composition's own material and must have preparation, event, and consequence.
- Avoid processing used to disguise weak composition, tuning, arrangement, or source design.

## Ableton implementation
- Document track roles, devices, routing, sidechain source, automation lanes, return chains, resampling origin, and render state.
- Preserve identical stem start/end points, sample rate, bit depth, and tail policy.
- Separate source-level advice from Ableton runtime verification.
- Never claim runtime validation without an observed Ableton test.

## Quality gates
- Artist self-review: tuning, groove, thematic hierarchy, variation, transitions, sound-source quality, low-end control, rights, and stem reconstruction.
- Arrangement: section causality, development, repetition, transition logic, signature event, climax, aftermath, and outro.
- Venom: musical identity, coherence, originality, catalogue function, and repeat-listening value.
- Irina production approval before mastering.
- Mastering rejects defects that require composition, arrangement, source, mix, timing, or rights changes.

## Week 1 album rule
- Plan the entire album as one system before producing final tracks.
- Define each track's theoretical role, tonal relationship, tempo relationship, transition compatibility, signature idea, and contrast function.
- Shared DNA must not become cloned arrangements.
- No track proceeds beyond proof stage until its theory map and role in the album are approved.
