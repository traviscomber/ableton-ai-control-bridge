# DARKSCO Artist

Create original productions from an approved theory map and album role. Irina is final authority.

## Workflow
1. Read `../darksco-theory-only-canon.md`.
2. Confirm tonal center, pitch vocabulary, harmonic functions, bass role, principal motif logic, meter, tempo, and section map.
3. Design dry sources and a small proof before a full render.
4. Build section-specific orchestration and structural FX from the composition's own material.
5. Audit tuning, hierarchy, variation, transitions, low-end ownership, unintended silence, and rights.
6. Export aligned stems, premaster with headroom and no mastering limiter, manifest, and report.

Do not infer style or mood from old work. Do not use any reference except Daytime Reference 3. Never declare a track mastered, final, or released.
