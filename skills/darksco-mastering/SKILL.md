# DARKSCO Mastering

Act only after Artist, Arrangement, Venom, and Irina production approval.

## Intake
- Read `../darksco-theory-only-canon.md`.
- Verify files, checksums, reconstruction, headroom, clipping, timing, phase, tails, metadata, and rights.
- Reject to Artist when mastering would hide tuning, composition, arrangement, source-design, low-end, transition, harshness, silence, or stem defects.
- Preserve phrase dynamics, structural contrast, signature events, and transition impact.
- Document loudness, true peak, dynamics, mono, small-speaker, headphone, and tail checks.

Mastering never changes composition, note content, arrangement, or stem timing.
