## DARKSCO Character Definition

### What is DARKSCO?

DARKSCO represents a fusion of dark disco aesthetics with funk-techno groove:

- **Dark** → Mystery, depth, introspection, industrial precision
- **Disco** → Groove, funk, soulful rhythm, dancefloor energy
- **Funk** → Rhythmic pocket, syncopation, soulful character
- **Techno** → Electronic elements, hypnotic repetition, modern production

The three variants express different moods of this unified aesthetic.
