# Night Protocol 001 — Premaster v1.1 Call-and-Response Revision

Status: **PREMASTER REVIEW — NOT MASTERED**

## Defect corrected

Irina rejected v1.0 because the track was not groovy enough, relied on one melody, and lacked an explicit question-and-answer structure.

## Changes

- Replaced the single music role with three distinct musical voices:
  - **Question motif**: short syncopated chord/melodic statement.
  - **Answer motif**: complementary phrase occupying the following musical space.
  - **Counter-hook**: introduced only in later sections for escalation and climax.
- Alternated question and answer by phrase instead of stacking them continuously.
- Removed the complete musical dialogue during the rupture section.
- Reintroduced all three voices in the late development and climax.
- Rebuilt the detail-percussion stem to react to the music:
  - shaker and hat accents yield under strong phrase attacks;
  - open-hat articulations appear mainly during answer phrases;
  - muted conga-like responses occupy syncopated gaps;
  - percussion density changes with section function.
- Preserved the clean v1.0 kick and bass stems.
- Preserved the 124 BPM, 160-bar arrangement and all other stems.
- Reconstructed the premaster with no limiter or mastering processing.
